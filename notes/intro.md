# Intro — Finance: The Friend We All Love to Hate (motivational, not graded)

- Politicians, media, Hollywood (Big Short, Wolf of Wall Street, Margin Call, Wall Street), economists (Zingales 2015: 57% of Economist readers disagree that financial innovation boosts growth; 48% of Americans say finance hurts the economy vs 34% benefits), even Wall Street hates finance.
- "Is finance really the villain?" → "Alright, let's talk."
- Finance = study of the optimal allocation of scarce FINANCIAL resources (branch of economics alongside agricultural, behavioral…).
- Finance tree: Procurement / Deployment / Distribution → Operational efficiency, Go/No-Go, Asset purchases & sales (valuation).
- The Virtuous Cycle is the heart of the financial system (financiers provide capital → firm procures assets → produce goods → revenue → net income → distributed or plowed back).
- A robust, efficient financial system (money, instruments, markets, institutions, central banks, regulators) promotes specialization and exchange.
- Fruits: progress images (old steam locomotive → Hong Kong skyline → Martin jetpack), tech giants, self-driving concept car.
- Goals of the day: Nu Skin operational efficiency + how Wall Street values Nu Skin (likely an in-class case).
