# Residential Solar mini-case (EMBA 520) — numbers used on the site

Source: instructor's case write-up and solutions workbook (not stored in this repo).

- Installed cost $28,792.50; cash discount $2,500 → net cost $26,292.50 at year 0
- Year 1 tax credits: federal 30% of net cost = $7,887.75; Utah 25% capped at $2,000 → $9,887.75
- Electricity produced: 96% of 14,014 kWh = 13,453.44 kWh; efficiency declines to 80% over 40 years (annual factor 0.8^(1/40) − 1 = −0.556%)
- Price $0.124/kWh in year 1, growing 4% a year; savings at year-end
- Added insurance $100 a year, growing 2%
- Discount rate 6.5%; 25-year horizon; no disposal or replacement costs
- Results (match workbook): NPV $9,685.82; IRR 10.93%; payback 9.06 years; discounted payback 13.77 years

Used on: Lecture 8 (scenario, sensitivity, simulation) and Lecture 6 Lab 1 preset.
Scenario, tornado (±20%) and simulation distributions are illustrative choices layered on the case.
The Lecture 3 "Flathead Lake" solar practice problem is a separate, simpler problem.
