# Lecture 4 — Bonds

## Narrative
Bonds = key tool for procuring capital and deploying it; bond markets larger than stock markets; set interest rates → drive GDP (Y = C + I + G + NX).
Road map: characteristics, risk factors, pricing, duration. Myths to dispel: "bonds are safe", "all bonds are the same".

## Characteristics
Shell Oil bond certificate: face (par) value, coupon rate, maturity date — "where is the yield to maturity?" (not on certificate; set by market)

## YTM = required return
Required return = Real return + E(Inflation) premium + Risk premium
= Risk-free rate + Risk premium (default, interest rate, liquidity, call, geopolitical, tax, others)
Big 3: inflation, default, interest rate risk

## Default risk
Moody's/S&P/Fitch ratings table (investment grade vs junk). Two ratings typical; $15–30K per rating; ratings ONLY measure default risk; downgrade = higher default risk.

## Pricing
Price = Σ Coupon/(1+r)^t + Face/(1+r)^M; Coupon = Par × coupon rate / payments per year; r = YTM / payments per year
- Price > par if coupon > YTM; < par if coupon < YTM; = par if equal
- Teeter-totter visual: YTM up ↔ price down; inflation, default, interest-rate risk push YTM up

## Interest rate risk & duration
Interest rate risk = price movement for a given change in YTM (lever length visual: fulcrum position)
Duration = weighted average time to receive PV of promised cash flows. Steps 1–8: periods, CFs, PV each, price, weight = PV/price, periods→years, multiply, sum.
Key points: longer maturity and lower coupon → longer duration; longer duration → bigger price swings; duration declines over bond's life.

## History / myths
- Late 70s/80s stagflation — broad yield impact (inflation)
- 2008 crisis — AAA MBS/CDOs, CDS, money market defaults; bond-specific vs broad
Taxonomies: issuer (sovereign, muni, corp, agency), rating (high-grade, low-grade, junk), attributes (callable, secured, zero-coupon)
