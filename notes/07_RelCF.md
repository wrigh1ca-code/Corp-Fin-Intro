# Lecture 7 — Relevant Cash Flows

## Narrative
Go/No-Go (Ch. 9 & 10). Recaps capital budgeting tools and NPV rule. Question: which cash flows are included/excluded?

## Guidance
- FCFF is the basis: OCF = EBIT(1−T) + Dep; − NCS; − ΔNWC. Financing side: creditors + shareholders.
- Incremental cash flow rule: include only if it occurs ONLY if project accepted; if partially, only the caused part.

## In or Out
IN: ΔNWC (recovered/"cleared" at end), side effects (erosion/complements), opportunity costs, taxes, final salvage value
OUT: sunk costs, financing costs

## Financing costs
- Interest/principal are cash flows to creditors — they are part of FCF, so don't subtract them.
- Preferred: include full purchase price/outlay at t=0 regardless of financing.
- Alternate ("fancy"): down payment at t=0, P&I later discounted at the LOAN rate, not project rate.
- Revisits Solar case (Lecture 3) to show it; tax benefit of debt captured in discount rate (WACC — future topic).

## Depreciation
GAAP vs MACRS; depreciation affects FCF only through taxes. If GAAP EBIT & taxes given, add back GAAP dep; if estimating EBIT & taxes, use MACRS throughout.

## Salvage value
After-tax salvage = Salvage − (Salvage − Book value) × T; Book = Cost − Acc. dep.
Example: cost 2.5M, acc dep 2.0M, salvage 1.5M, T 35% → BV 0.5M, gain 1.0M, tax 0.35M, ATSV 1.15M.
