# Lecture 2 — Percentage of Sales Forecasting

## Objectives
Forecast the income statement and balance sheet → needed to forecast FCFF (EBIT, Dep, NFA, NWC).

## Narrative link
Finance = optimal allocation of scarce resources (Procurement / Deployment / Distribution). V(asset) = f(utility); utility of financial assets = future FCFF. Re-shows FCF asset-side and financing-side tables.

## 8 steps of % of sales
1. Identify accounts that vary with sales (I/S: COGS, SG&A; B/S: most CA and CL)
2. Compute each as % of sales historically
3. Project sales (volume × price)
4. Forecast those accounts = forecast sales × %
5. Judgmentally estimate accounts that don't vary with sales
6. Calculate EFN or projected surplus
7. Determine plug variable
8. Use plug to balance statements

## Growth measures
- Internal growth rate = (ROA × b)/[1 − ROA × b] — max growth with retained earnings only
- Sustainable growth rate = (ROE × b)/[1 − ROE × b] — max growth with RE + debt holding D/E constant
- b = 1 − DPR; DPR = Dividends/Net income

## Key points / sanity checks
Sales forecast drives EVERYTHING ("if sales forecast is off, nothing else is right"). Which accounts vary: intuition + empirical data. Checks: reasonable sales? expense trends? assets sufficient? funding plan for EFN reasonable?

## Sales forecasting frameworks
- Top down: macro → industry → market share
- Bottom up: revenue streams / regions → query customers, sales force, managers → aggregate
- Driver paradigm: conceptual drivers → empirically test → estimate relation → forecast drivers → forecast revenue
- XOM example: ΔXOM_t = α + βΔOil_t; multivariate with ΔCPI, ΔGDP
