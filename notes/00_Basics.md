# Lecture 0 — The Basics of Asset Valuation (course backbone)

## Arc
1. Finance cycle (plowback) and finance tree: Procurement / Deployment / Distribution → Operational efficiency, Go/No-Go, Asset purchases & sales (valuation).
2. VALUE = Sum of Present Value of Expected Future CASH FLOWS.
3. "What is the primary driver of the value of any asset?" Car dealer → reservation price.
4. Ski-Doo 850 Summit (MSRP ~$14K): supply/demand — how many would buy at $5K, $10K, $15K, $20K?
5. Chain: Reservation price → Demand curve → Market price. (Houdini book story: Moon's Rare Books, $28K/$22K vs $100K buyers.)
6. What sets reservation price? "How much do you enjoy this?" → F(Expected Utility) → Demand → Price.
7. Consumption assets (car, jeans, lipstick, skis, Big Mac, AirPods) vs Financial assets (Apple stock certificate, Ford bond, IPO). Consumption utility = individual preferences; financial assets offer one utility: future cash flows.
8. F(Future Cash Flows) → Demand → Price.
9. Arrows attacking future cash flows: Inflation, Opportunity cost, Uncertainty → shield = PV = CF_t/(1+r)^t.
10. Keystone/arch + seer stone: PV = CF_t/(1+r)^t; Σ_{t=1}^{T} CF_t/(1+r)^t → Demand → Price.
11. Four questions: What cash flows? How to forecast? When is "T"? The right "r"?
12. Answer to #1 begins: FCFF (plowback cycle), FCFF formula.

## Framework mapping (proposed)
Σ_{t=1}^{T} CF_t / (1+r)^t
- CF — What cash flows? → L1 FCFF, L7 Relevant cash flows
- Forecast — How to forecast? → L2 % of sales; L8 scenario/sensitivity/simulation
- T — When is T? → L3 TVM (perpetuities), L5 terminal value & steady-state g
- r — The right r? → L3 TVM (discounting, APR/EAR), L4 required return/YTM, L9 WACC/CAPM
- Applications (Deployment): L4 Bonds, L5 Stocks (asset purchases & sales), L6 Capital budgeting (go/no-go)
